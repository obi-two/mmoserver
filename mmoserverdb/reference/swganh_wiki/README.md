# Reference data: the swganh wiki

Facts from wiki.swganh.org (the SWGANH developers' wiki, compiled from the
live game, the guides and players' screenshots; an approved source,
`docs/SOURCES.md`). Each row gives the page and the revision (`oldid`) it
was read from. Reference data: migrations take their values from here and
cite the page.

- `tuskens.csv`: the wiki's "A Tusken ... NPC (Example)" pages (revisions
  of 1 August 2010). The pages give no publish for their numbers; their
  sources are 2003 Allakhazam bestiary snapshots and SWG forum threads.
  `ham_min`/`ham_max` is the page's one HAM range (health, action and mind
  alike). `to_hit` is the page's percentage. `effectiveness` and
  `special_protection` are the armour block's EFF and SP entries, as
  `kind value` pairs; the pages' `VUL 0` entries (no protection) are left
  out. The wiki has no pages for the Tusken raider, sniper, warlord, King
  or bantha.
